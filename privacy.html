Essential local storage

03

This website may store certain preferences locally in your browser. This is used to remember settings such as your selected color theme, language and cookie preference.

These settings are stored locally on your device and are not used for advertising or cross-site tracking.

Items stored in your browser
Storage item Purpose
Theme preference Remembers whether you selected light or dark mode.

Cookie preference Remembers your choice regarding analytics.

Google Analytics

This website uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with the website and to improve its content and usability.

Google Analytics is only loaded after you explicitly choose to accept analytics. If you reject analytics, the Google Analytics script is not intentionally loaded by this website.

Google Analytics is provided by Google. Processing may involve the transfer of data to countries outside the European Economic Area where applicable safeguards and transfer mechanisms may be used.
